## Required P01–P10 review

The checker retains its ten review identities: P01 outcome/alternatives, P02 design/requirements, P03 source/consumer synchronization, P04 prevention/recovery, P05 durable knowledge, P06 understandable overview, P07 boundaries/compatibility, P08 recipient perspective, P09 simplicity/usability, P10 ownership/authority/efficiency. Several may share actual check evidence; this is not ten separate test suites.

For research, P06 can use an accurate notes summary rather than a dashboard; P08/P09 apply to clear reports and efficient workflows even without a UI. P01/P05/P10 remain applicable. Justify any truly N/A criteria by actual scope; missing evidence is not N/A. Residual scientific uncertainty in notes can coexist with a completed research criterion. An unresolved required review disposition/check cannot be silently waived.

## Checker behavior and compatibility

RT08 and the linked tool guide apply G02/G08 to tool choice, G05/G06/G09 to exact tool/source/output observations and G07/G10 to containment, effects and actual permissions. The source/lab/live/reporting workflow is unchanged. No catalogue entry grants installation, target access or a fallback around a refused capability.

- A material brief is frozen at begin; changing its bytes cannot be excused as exploratory freedom. Keep evolving notes separate. Widening the question/effects materially requires a new linked task.
- Final selected notes and finding/source files are bound by receipts. Correcting them is allowed, but invalidates old evidence until an applicable new check/receipt is recorded.
- The core's `pass` is the declared check result. It does not mean the hypothesis is true or that a vulnerability is absent. Define the correct research criterion before work.
- Missing runtime or evidence blocks dependent verified completion. It does not forbid independent authorized thinking/source work that remains within its own boundaries.
- The runtime is a local evidence-binding gate. It provides no auto-approval policy, live network adapter, backend or scheduler. The manifest's runtime digests pin compatibility; they do not authenticate execution or authorize a target.
