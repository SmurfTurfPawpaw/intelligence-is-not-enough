# Illustrative brief — populate an actual task before using

Question: What behavior does an owned synthetic ledger show when the same operation identifier is submitted twice, compared with a new identifier?

Beneficiary/outcome: The researcher gains reproducible evidence to accept or reject a duplicate-acceptance hypothesis. Authority: only a researcher-owned temporary local fixture with synthetic identifiers; no network, real ledger, customer records or durable business effect.

EX-01: The investigation SHALL capture an actual repeated-identifier observation and a meaningful new-identifier control from the local fixture, preserving its exact source, method and output.

EX-02: The notes SHALL distinguish hypothesis, observation, interpretation and remaining limits. Acceptable outcome: a supported, falsified or bounded inconclusive duplicate-acceptance hypothesis, provided the stated experiments/evidence and controls are complete and reliable. Missing experiments/evidence remain incomplete.

Approach: choose or construct the smallest synthetic state-machine fixture; experiment order and alternative explanations may evolve in separate notes. A materially different question, environment, effect or budget requires a linked new task.

Budget/stop: at most one investigator, one bounded local session and three attempted fixture executions; stop on uncontained I/O or unexpected non-synthetic data. Recovery: terminate the fixture; retain evidence and dispose only the owned scratch data according to the local test policy. No live-system conclusion, vulnerability-free claim or report submission follows from this result.

Evidence: exact fixture source, actual observed output, attributed notes, required workflow review/receipts and final gate. Check the current actual source and selected files; no freshness/liveness assertion is made about another environment.
